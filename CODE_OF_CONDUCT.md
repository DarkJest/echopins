# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy. Most people here are doing this in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
whenever someone is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report abuse privately to the maintainers via GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code and issues that violate this document, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate. Responses are
proportionate: a first misstep normally gets a private word, not a ban.

Maintainers who do not follow this document in good faith may face consequences decided by other
maintainers.

## A note specific to this project

EchoPins carries people's voices. If a bug report or feature request involves a recording of
someone, do not attach the audio, do not quote what was said, and do not identify the speaker.
Describe the behaviour instead.
